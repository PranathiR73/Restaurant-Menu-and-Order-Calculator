Welcome to Mysore Spice Restaurant!

Mysore Spice is a vegetarian restaurant inspired by the traditional food and flavours of Mysuru, Karnataka.

We serve popular South Indian dishes, delicious street chaats, tasty snacks, traditional sweets and refreshing drinks at affordable prices.

Our Specialities

  • Mysore Masala Dosa and Idli Vada
  • Bisi Bele Bath, Pongal and Poori Sagu
  • Pani Puri, Masala Puri and Bhel Puri
  • Mysore Pak and Gulab Jamun
  • Filter Coffee, Badam Milk and Fresh Juices

Our Aim

Our aim is to serve tasty vegetarian food, offer friendly service and make every customer feel welcome.

Visit our menu, choose your favourite dishes and calculate your bill easily.
